What makes a good meeting agenda

An agenda is a contract for how the meeting will use everyone's time. The best ones share four things: the goal (what we'll have decided or produced by the end), the topics that serve that goal and nothing else, an owner for each item, and a time box so the meeting stays on track. Send it in advance so people arrive prepared rather than warming up live. For the deeper how-to, see our effective meeting agendas guide.
