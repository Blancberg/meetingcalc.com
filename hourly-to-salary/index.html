How to convert hourly pay to an annual salary

The formula is: annual salary = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks worked per year. For standard full-time work that's 40 hours across 52 weeks, or 2,080 hours a year. So $25 an hour works out to $52,000 a year before taxes.

If your hourly role isn't paid for holidays, reduce the weeks worked (or use the toggle) to compare it fairly against a salaried job that is paid year-round.

Why this matters for meetings

Once you know the hourly value of the people in a room, the cost of a meeting becomes obvious. A one-hour meeting with eight people earning $40/hour is $320 of paid time — before any benefits or overhead.
